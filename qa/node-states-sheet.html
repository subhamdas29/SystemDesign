Attribute Proposal A (Architectural Micro-Badges) Proposal B (Typographic Pips & Accents)

No-Box Fidelity 100% free-floating on canvas. No cards, borders, or backgrounds. 100% free-floating on canvas. No cards, borders, or backgrounds.
Motion Affordance Subtle brightness lift on hover + dashed underline; pulsing badge on Overloaded. 1.05x subtle scale on hover + orange bullet entry; pulsing lightning on Overloaded.
Recommendation (Recommended) Highest fidelity to Figma's minimal drafting aesthetic; Cream selection preserves orange purely for threats/active tabs. High-impact console style; makes active node selection immediately pop out on dark canvas.
